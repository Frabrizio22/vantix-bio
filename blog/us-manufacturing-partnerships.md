## Three Supply Chain Models in Research Peptides

The peptide market operates on three distinct models:

### 1. Overseas Import (6-12 week cycles)
**Flow:** Order from Chinese manufacturer → wait 4-8 weeks → import → third-party testing (2-3 weeks) → list for sale

**Pros:** Lower per-unit cost at scale (1,000+ vials)  
**Cons:** Long lead times, import risk, limited manufacturing visibility

### 2. White-Label Distribution (instant availability)
**Flow:** Buy pre-tested inventory from US distributor → relist with your branding

**Pros:** No wait time, someone else handled testing  
**Cons:** 3-4x wholesale markup passed to customers, zero manufacturing insight

### 3. Direct Domestic Partnership (3-4 week cycles)
**Flow:** Order from US manufacturer → 7-14 day synthesis → third-party testing (10 days) → list for sale

**Pros:** Shorter supply chain, manufacturing visibility, fair pricing  
**Cons:** Higher per-unit cost than overseas bulk, requires direct lab relationships

**We chose model 3.**

- **Consistent purity** (>99% HPLC across batches)

**Supply Chain Advantages:**

1. **Traceability** - We can audit manufacturing records, track raw material sources, and verify synthesis protocols
2. **Regulatory visibility** - US facilities operate under FDA oversight and enforceable quality standards
3. **Faster response** - If a batch fails testing or we need a rush order, turnaround is days (not months)
4. **Lower minimums** - We order 50-100 vials per SKU instead of 500+ bulk imports

**Why domestic synthesis matters:**

Where a peptide is manufactured has practical implications beyond nationalism. US-based facilities provide:

- **Enforceable standards** - Labor, environmental, and safety regulations you can verify
- **Shorter supply chains** - Fewer handoffs = less contamination risk
- **Direct auditing** - We can visit facilities, review synthesis logs, inspect equipment
- **Legal recourse** - If something goes wrong, we have contractual protections

### Independent Lab Testing (Janoshik)

Every batch goes to **Janoshik Analytical** in the Czech Republic for third-party verification:

- **HPLC-DAD** (purity quantification)
- **LC-MS/MS** (molecular identity confirmation)
- **LAL endotoxin screening** (bacterial contamination check)

**Turnaround:** 7-10 business days from sample shipment to published COA.

**Why Janoshik specifically?**

1. **Industry standard** - Most peptide researchers recognize and trust their COAs
2. **Public verification** - Every report is verifiable at janoshik.com/results using the task ID
3. **Comprehensive testing** - They run the full panel (identity + purity + endotoxin) in one submission

## Why Not Manufacture In-House?

**Short answer:** The economics don't make sense at our current scale.

**Building a CGMP peptide synthesis lab requires:**

- $500k-1M in equipment (HPLC, lyophilizer, fume hoods, cold storage)
- $200k+/year in compliance costs (inspections, certifications, documentation)
- $150k+/year in specialized labor (synthetic chemists, QC analysts)
- 12-18 months to get operational

**At our revenue ($4-6k/month), we'd need to:**

- Raise prices 40-60% to cover fixed costs
- Sacrifice third-party verification (since we'd be testing our own product)
- Tie up capital in inventory instead of using it for marketing/growth

**Our current model:**

- Keeps prices low (no fixed overhead)
- Preserves third-party credibility (Janoshik independence)
- Lets us focus on customer experience instead of GMP compliance

**If we hit $100k/month revenue, we'll revisit in-house manufacturing.** Until then, partnering with established manufacturers is the smarter play.

## Common Questions

### "Isn't overseas peptide manufacturing cheaper?"

**Yes, but only at massive scale.**

If you're ordering 1,000+ vials per SKU, Chinese manufacturers offer better per-unit pricing (~$3-5/vial vs. $7-10/vial US).

**But:**

- You need 6-12 weeks lead time
- You're locked into high minimums
- Import risk (customs, quality variance)
- Longer time to market = more capital tied up

**For a vendor at our scale (50-200 vials/month per SKU), US manufacturing is faster and more flexible.**

### "How do I know your manufacturer is legit?"

**We don't disclose manufacturer names publicly** (competitive reasons), but:

1. **Every batch gets Janoshik tested** - COAs are public and verifiable
2. **Purity is consistent** - Our Tirz batches average 99.3-99.8%, not wild variance
3. **No contamination** - Endotoxin levels consistently <5 EU/mg
4. **We've been ordering from the same partners for 18+ months** - No quality issues

**If the COAs show >99% purity and pass endotoxin screening every time, the manufacturer is doing their job.**

### "Why not use a US lab instead of Janoshik?"

**Three reasons:**

1. **Industry recognition** - Researchers trust Janoshik COAs more than unknown US labs
2. **Public verification** - Every report is verifiable at janoshik.com/results (most US labs don't offer this)
3. **Cost** - Janoshik charges $250-350 for the full panel. US labs charge $400-600 for the same tests.

**We prioritize verifiability over domestic testing.** If a researcher can't independently confirm the COA, it doesn't build trust.
