## Days 3-10: Synthesis

The manufacturer runs **solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS)** to build Tirzepatide's 39-amino-acid chain.

**What happens during synthesis:**

1. **Amino acid coupling** - Building the peptide chain one residue at a time
2. **Protecting group removal** - Cleaning off chemical guards after each step
3. **Cleavage from resin** - Releasing the finished peptide
4. **Purification (HPLC)** - Removing synthesis byproducts and incomplete chains
5. **Lyophilization** - Freeze-drying into stable powder form
6. **Vial filling** - Precisely measured 30mg doses per vial under sterile conditions

**Why this takes 7-14 days:**

- Each coupling cycle needs 2-4 hours
- Purification requires multiple HPLC runs to hit >99% purity
- Lyophilization takes 24-48 hours
- QC checks happen at every step

**What we DON'T do:** Rush synthesis. Faster = lower purity. We wait for it to be done right.

## Day 11: Sampling for Janoshik

We pull **3 vials randomly** from the batch for third-party verification.

**Why 3 vials?**

- 1 vial for HPLC purity testing
- 1 vial for LC-MS identity confirmation  
- 1 vial for LAL endotoxin screening

**Some vendors only send 1 vial.** If that sample is damaged in transit or gets contaminated, they have to reorder and wait another week.

**We send 3** so Janoshik has backups if needed.

**Shipping:** FedEx overnight to Janoshik Analytical (Czech Republic).  
**Tracking:** We monitor until "Delivered" confirmation.

## Days 12-21: Janoshik Testing

**Day 12:** Janoshik receives samples, assigns Task ID **#165872**

**Day 13-16:** HPLC purity analysis

- Sample reconstituted in sterile water
- Injected into HPLC column
- UV detector measures peptide purity at 230nm wavelength
- **Result:** 99.42% pure (target: ≥98%)

**Day 17-19:** LC-MS/MS identity confirmation

- Mass spectrometry confirms molecular weight matches Tirzepatide
- Fragmentation pattern verifies amino acid sequence
- **Result:** Identity confirmed (m/z matches expected 4813.5 g/mol)

**Day 20-21:** LAL endotoxin screening

- Limulus Amebocyte Lysate test (detects bacterial contamination)
- **Result:** 1.8 EU/vial (target: <10 EU/vial)

**Day 22:** Janoshik publishes full COA at janoshik.com/results  
**We receive:** PDF report via email + public verification link

## What Makes This Different from Other Vendors

### Most vendors operate on 8-12 week cycles:

1. Order 500-1,000 vials from China (4-8 weeks)
2. Wait for customs/import (1-2 weeks)
3. Send for testing (2-3 weeks)
4. List for sale

**Stockout window:** If they sell out in week 6, customers wait 6-8 more weeks for the next batch.

**Capital requirement:** $3,000-5,000 tied up per SKU (500+ vials)

### We operate on 3-4 week cycles:

1. Order 50-100 vials from US manufacturer (7-14 days)
2. Send for testing (same week delivery)
3. Janoshik testing (7-10 days)
4. List for sale

**Stockout window:** If we sell out early, next batch is already in production (1-2 week gap max)

**Capital requirement:** $800-1,200 per SKU (50-100 vials)

**Trade-off:** Higher per-unit cost ($8-10/vial vs. $3-5/vial overseas)

**Benefit:** Consistent availability, faster response to demand

## Related Questions

**Q: Why don't you manufacture in-house?**

Building a CGMP peptide synthesis lab costs $500k-1M+ (equipment, compliance, labor). At our current scale ($4-6k/month revenue), we'd need to raise prices 40-60% to cover fixed costs. Partnering with established manufacturers keeps pricing fair.

**Q: What if Janoshik is delayed?**

We build buffer time into restocks. If testing takes 12 days instead of 10, we still hit our target date. If there's a major delay (2+ weeks), we update the product page with the new ETA.

**Q: Do you ever skip testing to save money?**

Never. Every batch gets the full panel (HPLC + LC-MS + endotoxin). No exceptions. Testing costs $305/batch, but it's the entire foundation of trust.

**Q: Can I see your manufacturer's facility?**

We don't disclose manufacturer names publicly (competitive reasons), but every batch's purity consistency proves they're legit. Our Tirz batches average 99.3-99.8% purity across 9 batches. If synthesis quality was inconsistent, you'd see 97%, 94%, 99% wild swings.
