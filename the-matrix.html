From Material to Report

Every batch goes through the same six steps. Testing happens twice, once on the incoming material and once on the finished product, and the finished-batch results are the ones we publish.

  1. Step 1

    Imported peptide material

    The peptide material is made by a separate manufacturer and imported to the U.S.

  2. Step 2

    Incoming material testing

    The incoming peptide material is tested by a laboratory accredited to ISO/IEC 17025 before it is used. This confirms what goes into the batch before we build on it.

  3. Step 3

    Finished in the U.S.

    Each batch is formulated, filled, and freeze-dried in a U.S. laboratory.

  4. Step 4

    Finished product testing

    A random, representative sample from each finished batch is sent blind to an independent third-party laboratory for purity by HPLC, identity confirmation, and peptide content per vial. This confirms what is in the finished vial.

  5. Step 599% minimum purity

    Release standard

    A batch is released for sale only after independent testing shows it meets our requirements, including a minimum of 99% purity. Any batch outside our standard is listed as such, with its COA public.

  6. Step 6

    Verify your batch

    Every batch has its own batch number. Scan the QR code on your vial to open our verification portal, or go to the portal on our website and type in the batch number. The results show that batch’s public certificate of analysis (COA), with a direct link to the original report on the laboratory’s own website.
