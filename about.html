Why Vantix Bio Exists

Third-party testing has become common in the research peptide market. But a certificate for a whole product line, or for an earlier batch, can't tell you what's in the vial you received. You shouldn't have to take it on faith that the label matches the report.

We wanted to make that connection clearer. Each Vantix Bio production batch receives its own batch identity. A sample from each sealed production batch goes to an independent third-party laboratory, and the results are tied to that specific batch.

When your order arrives, the batch number on the vial gives you a direct path back to those results.

Independent Testing, Batch by Batch

We don't rely on a supplier's certificate alone. Every production batch is tested by an independent third-party laboratory before release. Each report names the laboratory, the analytical results and the method. One batch, one report, never one certificate reused across batches.

If you can't verify it, you shouldn't sell it. That's the entire philosophy.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell on a manufacturer's word. A supplier's certificate is a claim. A third-party result on our own batch is evidence.
  • We don't cherry-pick batches. Every batch gets tested, not just the ones that look good.
  • We don't ask you to trust our PDFs. Each report links straight to the lab's own page for that batch, so you can check it at the source. No report number to copy over and type in.
  • We don't hide results. Reports are public, with no login and no request form.
  • We don't call it “representative testing.” One test per product line says nothing about the next batch. Batch-to-batch variability is real, so every batch is tested.

Batch-level testing shouldn't be a premium feature.
