What testing does each batch receive?
Every batch undergoes independent third-party verification. Complete analytical reports are published with verifiable task IDs and batch-specific QR codes.
02
How do I verify a COA?
Enter your VX-[PRODUCT]-XXX batch ID (printed on vial label) into the Verification Portal. The full analytical report displays instantly with direct links to lab verification pages.

What does "Research Use Only" mean?
All Vantix Bio compounds are sold strictly for in vitro and in vivo laboratory research for research use only. Not for human consumption, clinical use, or therapeutic purposes.
04
Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship within the United States only. International shipping introduces regulatory complexity and delays that compromise batch traceability.
05
How long until my order ships?
Orders typically ship next business day via USPS. Shipping takes 3-5 business days depending on location. You receive tracking immediately after shipment.
