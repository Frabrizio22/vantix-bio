Is Vantix Bio Legitimate?

Yes. Here's how to verify it yourself.

How Our Verification System Works

Every Vantix Bio vial includes a unique batch identifier and QR code. Scan it, and you'll see the complete third-party Certificate of Analysis (COA) for that specific batch.

What You Can Verify:

  • Purity (HPLC): Percentage of target peptide vs impurities
  • Identity (LC-MS): Molecular weight confirmation
  • Quantity: Actual mass in the vial
  • Endotoxin Testing: LAL assay results (when applicable)
  • Batch Traceability: Link between your vial and the tested sample

Third-Party Testing (ISO 17025 Accredited)

We don't test our own products. Every batch is analyzed by an independent laboratory with ISO 17025 accreditation—the international standard for testing competence.

Why this matters: The lab testing our peptides has no financial relationship with us beyond performing the tests. They don't benefit from inflating results or passing contaminated batches.

California-Based Supply Chain

Our peptides are:

  • Synthesized in California (Orange County)
  • Tested by a Southern California ISO 17025 lab
  • Fulfilled from Santa Cruz, California

Your peptide never leaves California until it ships to you.

How to Verify Your Batch

  1. Locate the batch ID on your vial label (format: VX-XXXX-001)
  2. Go to vantixbio.com/verify
  3. Enter the batch ID or scan the QR code
  4. View the complete third-party COA

If the batch ID doesn't return a valid COA, contact us at support@vantixbio.com immediately.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't manufacture peptides ourselves (we partner with certified California manufacturers)
  • We don't test our own batches (third-party labs only)
  • We don't show "sample" COAs—every batch gets its own report
  • We don't fabricate testimonials or fake reviews

Common Questions

Why don't you manufacture in-house?

We focus on quality verification, customer service, and education. Our California manufacturing partners specialize in peptide synthesis and hold ISO certifications. This allows us to leverage established expertise rather than building our own facility.

How do I know the COA matches my vial?

Each vial is labeled with a batch-specific ID. When you verify that ID, the COA you see was generated from a sample taken from the same manufacturing batch as your vial. The QR code and batch ID create an auditable link between the physical product and the lab report.

What if my batch doesn't verify?

Contact support@vantixbio.com with your order number and batch ID. We'll investigate immediately. A non-verifying batch ID is a serious issue and will be resolved or refunded.
