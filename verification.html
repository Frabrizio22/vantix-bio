Common Questions

Why don't you manufacture in-house?

We focus on quality verification and education. Our California manufacturing partners specialize in peptide synthesis and hold cGMP certifications. This leverages established expertise rather than building our own facility.

How do I know the COA matches my vial?

Each vial is labeled with a batch-specific ID. The COA you see was generated from a sample taken from the same manufacturing batch. The QR code creates an auditable link between the physical product and lab report.

What does cGMP-aligned mean?

cGMP (current Good Manufacturing Practices) is the FDA framework for pharmaceutical manufacturing. "Aligned" means the facility follows these practices without claiming FDA drug approval (our products are Research Use Only).

What if my batch doesn't verify?

Contact support@vantixbio.com with your order number and batch ID. We'll investigate immediately. A non-verifying batch is a serious issue and will be resolved or refunded.
