FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What buyers ask first.

The questions we hear most often about batch verification and research peptide quality.

What does Janoshik-verified mean? +
Janoshik is an independent EU-based laboratory specializing in peptide testing. Every batch we receive is sent to Janoshik for third-party verification before release. They test HPLC purity, LC-MS identity confirmation, and endotoxin levels. Nothing ships until we receive passing results.
Do you ship internationally? +
Currently we ship within the United States only. International shipping is complex due to varying customs regulations for research compounds. We're exploring international options for future expansion.
How do I verify my COA? +
Every vial includes a QR code linking to our verification page. You can also visit vantixbio.com/verify and enter your batch ID (printed on the vial label). This displays the full Janoshik certificate, HPLC chart, and test results for your specific batch.
Are these for human consumption? +
No. All products are sold for laboratory research use only. They are not FDA-approved for human consumption, clinical use, or therapeutic purposes. These are research-grade compounds intended for qualified researchers and institutions.
