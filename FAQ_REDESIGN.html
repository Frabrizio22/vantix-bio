Documentation

Common questions.

01
What testing does each batch receive?
Every batch undergoes independent third-party verification: HPLC-DAD (purity quantification), LC-MS/MS (molecular identity), and LAL endotoxin screening (bacterial contamination check). Reports are published with verifiable task IDs.
02
How do I verify a COA?
Enter your VX-[PRODUCT]-XXX batch ID (printed on vial label) into the Verification Portal. The full analytical report displays instantly with direct links to lab verification pages.
03
What does "Research Use Only" mean?
All Vantix Bio compounds are sold strictly for in vitro and in vivo laboratory research under the RUO exemption. Not for human consumption, clinical use, or therapeutic purposes.
04
Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship within the United States only. International shipping introduces regulatory complexity and delays that compromise batch traceability.
05
How long until my order ships?
In-stock orders ship same-day or next business day. USPS Priority Mail typically delivers in 2-3 business days. You receive tracking immediately after shipment.
